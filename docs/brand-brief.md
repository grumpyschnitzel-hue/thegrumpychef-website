1. **The Rebel** — Sarcastic, defiant. Calls out BS. Earns attention.
2. **The Creator** — Obsessive about craft and detail. Builds authority.
3. **The Sage** — Brutally honest, fatherly wisdom. Builds trust.

Every piece of content uses 1-2 of these voices. Never all three at once.

**Externally:** Master Chef who rebuilt from collapse, teaching operators systems that work.
**Internally:** AI-powered delivery (Claude Code, n8n, custom tools) — 10x faster than anyone in the space. The AI is NEVER the pitch. The 20 years of context IS the product.

## The Signature Framework

### 21-Day Restaurant Recovery Protocol
The foundation everything references. Three 7-day phases:

| Phase | Days | Focus |
|-------|------|-------|
| **Stabilize** | 1-7 | Stop the bleeding. Find the #1 profit leak. Emergency fixes. |
| **Systematize** | 8-14 | Build SOPs, tracking systems, accountability structures. |
| **Scale** | 15-21 | Optimize for growth. Menu engineering. Pricing. Labor. |

- 72-Hour Profit Discovery — emergency diagnostic (find the leak in 3 days)
- Operator Life OS — personal systems for burnt-out operators (5 life systems)

## Content Strategy

### Philosophy (Koe + Hormozi + Search-Based)
- 1 deep essay/week from lived experience → blog + newsletter
- Slice each essay into 3-5 LinkedIn posts + 1 carousel
- Blog = search-based (long shelf life, compounds over time) — 12 articles live
- LinkedIn = short shelf life but builds authority
- Newsletter = weekly broadcast (same essay, adapted for email)

### Content Mix: 50-30-20
- 50% value (education, frameworks, tools)
- 30% engagement (stories, vulnerability, questions)
- 20% promotional (offers, CTAs, social proof)

### Writing Formula
Failure → Lie exposed → Turn → Lesson → Proof → Offer

# Editor Brief Template

Standard instructions for the external video editor.
Customize per-project, but this is the baseline.

## Brand Guidelines for Editor

### Visual Identity
- **Primary color:** Navy (#0A1628)
- **Accent color:** Gold (#D4AF37)
- **Text color:** White (#FFFFFF)
- **Font (lower thirds):** Oswald Bold
- **Font (body text overlays):** Inter Regular/Medium

### Style Rules
- **Professional but raw.** Not over-produced. Not corporate. Real.
- **No stock B-roll.** Use actual footage or simple text slides.
- **Music:** Minimal. No stock motivational tracks. Subtle instrumental during transitions only.
- **Transitions:** Clean cuts preferred. No fancy transitions. Occasional crossfade okay.
- **Lower thirds:** Navy background, gold accent bar (4px), Oswald Bold white text.
- **Pace:** Natural speaking pace. Don't speed-up-edit to make Chris talk faster. The deliberate pace IS the brand.

### What To Cut
- Dead air (already removed in clean cut, but double-check)
- Any remaining "um"/"uh" that break flow
- Off-topic tangents unless they serve the story
- Visible distractions (notifications, interruptions)

### What To Keep
- Pauses for emphasis (these are intentional)
- Raw reactions and genuine moments
- The accent. Don't "clean up" the German-English speaking style.
- Kitchen/cooking B-roll if available
- Any moment where Chris gets visibly passionate — that's gold

### Deliverables Expected
1. `final_cut.mp4` — Full edited video, ready for YouTube/LinkedIn
2. (Optional) Suggested clip timestamps for shorts

### File Specs
- Resolution: 1920x1080 (16:9) minimum
- Codec: H.264
- Bitrate: 8-15 Mbps
- Audio: AAC, 48kHz, stereo
- Format: .mp4

### Shorts Specs (if editor creates them)
- Resolution: 1080x1920 (9:16) for TikTok/Reels/Shorts
- Or: 1080x1080 (1:1) for LinkedIn/Instagram feed
- Must have hook in first 3 seconds
- Caption-ready (leave space for text overlays)

"I didn't work harder. I built systems. Recovery frameworks applied to a restaurant. Same logic. Different kitchen."

In 2022 I was $370,000 in debt running a seasonal kitchen at the edge of the Canadian Yukon. 145-day season. 80-hour weeks. No safety net. I had been white-knuckling it for years and had nothing to show for it except exhaustion and a collapsing operation.

Getting sober showed me what was actually broken. Not the people — the absence of systems. Pre-decided responses to every recurring situation. No exceptions. No negotiation. Behavior leads belief.

64°N Weekly is what I wish I had when everything was falling apart. One honest story. One thing that works. Every Tuesday — from the same place I rebuilt everything.
